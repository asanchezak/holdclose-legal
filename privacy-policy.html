HoldClose Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

The short version

Your relationships, journals, reflections and reminders are stored on this device. If you turn on AI help, the text needed to write or rewrite a message is sent to OpenAI to generate it and then returned. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and there is no tracking of you across other apps or websites.

What stays on this device

Everything you record: the people you hold close, your notes about them, reflections, journal entries, memory-bank answers, logged moments, reminders, badges, and any photos you add. It is kept in the app's own storage on this device. We do not hold a copy.

What leaves the device, and when

Only if you have turned AI help on, and only to generate text — a suggested message, a rewrite, guidance, a check-in or an analysis. Some of these are prepared as a screen opens rather than waiting for a tap, so opening a person's screen can be what sends the request. That request includes the name you saved for that person, your notes about the relationship, your goal for it, the communication style and example messages you saved, recently logged moments, your reflection ratings, and how long it has been since you last logged something. It also includes your own first name and anything you wrote in your profile about your personality, what matters to you and how you usually text, which is what makes a suggestion sound like you. Nothing is sent when you are simply using the rest of the app, and nothing at all is sent while AI help is off.

Your choice, in two parts

Before anything is generated, HoldClose asks you once, names OpenAI, and lists what would be sent. Nothing is sent unless you agree. Your journal entries and your memory-bank answers are a second, separate choice that is off unless you turn it on — they are not included in the first. Both switches live in Settings › Privacy and either can be turned off at any time, which stops future requests immediately. Turning off AI help turns off the journal and memory setting with it.

Who receives it

Two companies. Supabase hosts the small server the app talks to, the anonymous identifier below, and a counter of how many generation requests you have made. OpenAI receives the request through that server, generates the text and returns it. There are no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting services in this app.

Your identity here

There is no account. No email address, no password, nothing to sign in to. On first launch the app is given an anonymous identifier, which exists so the server can tell one device's generation requests from another's and apply a fair-use limit. It is not connected to your name, your email or your contacts.

Contacts

HoldClose does not ask for access to your contacts and cannot read your address book. When you choose someone, iOS shows you its own contact picker and hands back only the person you picked. HoldClose keeps a copy of their name, one phone number, one email address and their photo, on this device, so it can show them to you and let you reach them. Nobody else in your address book is seen. The name you saved for a person you chose is included in generation requests about them — their first name where iOS gave one, and otherwise the full name as it stands in your address book.

Keeping and deleting

Settings › Privacy › Delete My Data removes everything: every person, reflection, journal entry, reminder, badge and saved memory on this device, every setting you have chosen, and the anonymous identifier itself, which is deleted from our server so that nothing connects this device to anything. It asks once and cannot be undone. You do not have to write to us to have it done, and there is no copy held anywhere to restore from. Deleting a single person removes their reflections, journal entries, reminders, activity, badges and saved memories with them, and deleting the app removes everything local. On the server side the content of a generation request is not stored: only the request counter, which holds a number rather than any text and is cleared within a couple of days. OpenAI's own handling of the request is governed by its API terms. Withdrawing your agreement stops anything further being sent, but cannot recall a request already made.

Purchases

Hold Close Premium is sold by Apple, not by us. We never see or store your payment details: the app asks the App Store whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account and keeps nothing beyond that yes or no. Nothing about what you buy is sent to our server, and a subscription is not used to identify you.

Who we are

HoldClose is published by Kris Asleson. Questions about this policy and anything else privacy-related can be sent to kris.asleson@gmail.com. Deleting what the app holds does not need an email: it is a button in Settings › Privacy.

Children

HoldClose is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the app and the date above changes with it. Questions can be sent to kris.asleson@gmail.com, or through Contact Support on the Support screen, which writes to the same address.
