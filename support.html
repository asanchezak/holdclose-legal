HoldClose Support

HoldClose helps you keep track of the people who matter to you, and suggests things you might say to them.

Getting help

Write to kris.asleson@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — and which iPhone and iOS version you are on, which you can find in Settings › General › About. You can also reach the same address from inside the app, under Settings › Contact Support, which fills those details in for you.

Turning AI help off

Settings › Privacy, inside the app. Turning it off stops anything further being sent, immediately. Journals and memory-bank answers are a separate switch that is off unless you turn it on.

Your contacts

HoldClose cannot read your address book. When you choose someone, iOS shows you its own contact picker and hands back only the person you picked.

Deleting your data

Deleting a person in the app removes their reflections, journal entries, reminders, activity, badges and saved memories with them. Deleting the app removes everything it holds on the device. To have anything on our side removed, write to the address above.

Reporting a problem with generated text

Suggestions are written by a language model and can be wrong or inappropriate. Nothing is sent on your behalf — you always read it first. If something came out badly, send it to the address above and say what you asked for.
